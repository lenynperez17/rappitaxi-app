NEGOTIATE YOUR FARE
Forget surge pricing that doubles your cost when you need a ride the most. With our InDrive-style negotiation system, you set the amount you think is fair. Nearby drivers receive your request and can accept your price or propose a new one. You choose who to ride with.

SERVICES FOR EVERY NEED
- Express: Quick and affordable rides for your daily commute.
- Executive: Mid-range vehicles for meetings and appointments.

- Freight: Send packages quickly and securely.
- Intercity: Travel between cities with verified drivers.
- Scheduled: Book your ride in advance and travel stress-free.

SAFETY ON EVERY RIDE
Your peace of mind is our priority. Every ride includes:
- Real-time GPS tracking so your loved ones can follow your journey.
- Emergency SOS button with direct access to help services.
- Verified drivers with validated documentation.
- Two-way rating system.
- In-app chat to communicate with your driver without sharing your personal number.

PAY YOUR WAY
Total payment flexibility:
- Cash: Pay the driver directly.
- MercadoPago: Pay digitally, quickly, and securely.
- Yape and Plin: Scan the QR and transfer directly to the driver.
- Rapi Wallet: Top up your balance and pay with no extra fees.
- Card: Debit or credit securely saved.

FEATURES THAT MAKE THE DIFFERENCE
- Favorite places: Save home, work, and frequent destinations to request rides in seconds.
- Full history: Review all your previous trips with route details, price, and driver info.
- Ratings: Check your rating history and maintain a strong community reputation.
- Push notifications: Get real-time alerts about your ride status, driver offers, and more.
- Scheduled rides: Plan your transportation in advance so you are never late.

FOR DRIVERS
If you are a driver, Rapi Team offers a transparent platform:
- Receive requests from nearby passengers and choose which ones to accept.
- Negotiate fair rates that value your time and fuel.
- Detailed earnings dashboard with daily, weekly, and monthly stats.
- Digital wallet to manage your income.
- Integrated navigation to reach your destination via the best route.
- Simple and accessible credit system.

A GROWING COMMUNITY
Rapi Team was born with a clear mission: to create a fairer transportation system where passengers and drivers benefit mutually. No abusive intermediaries, no inflated fares, no surprises.
